🎯 Purpose of LiftMate Car Repair

LiftMate autonomously and remotely navigates under vehicles using 360° Mecanum wheels, live camera vision, and dual electric actuators (Jack & Holder) to lift and stabilize cars safely—eliminating the need for human mechanics to enter hazardous under-load crush zones.

♻️ Sustainability & Environmental Impact

  • 100% Zero-Emission Electric Drive: Battery-electric Mecanum motors & actuators produce 0 g exhaust emissions vs. heavy diesel recovery trucks.
  • Zero Hydraulic Oil Contamination: Electric linear actuators (Jack & Holder) eliminate hydraulic fluid leaks into roads, soil, and groundwater.
  • Lower Carbon Footprint: On-site robotic assistance avoids tow-truck dispatch (~4.2 kg CO₂ saved per vehicle repair) and extends tire & chassis life.
  • Sustainable Worker Safety: Zero back strain and zero under-vehicle crush exposure via remote smartphone telemetry.
